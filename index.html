About our school

Sabatia Forest Primary and Junior Secondary School provides a learning environment where every learner is encouraged to participate, develop their abilities and work towards their potential.

📘

Learning first

We encourage curiosity, strong foundational skills, practical learning and continuous improvement.

🤝

Whole-child growth
